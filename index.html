History Heroes Jam!

The object of the game is to end with one of your cards on the tile with the game Setup: The hand of cards on the left is Player 1, the hand of cards on the right is Player 2 When it is your turn, click on one of your cards in your hand. The black squares show you where you can play. The arrows on the card allow you to push your opponents card in that direction. If two arrows are pushing against each other neither card will move, unless on the arrows is a double arrow. The picnic blanket tiles are the playable spaces. Cards cannot be placed on the grass, but they can be pushed onto the grass. No cards can go in the dirt.
